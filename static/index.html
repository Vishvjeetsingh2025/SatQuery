Why SatQuery AI

One assistant. Every satellite question.

A hybrid Vision-Language + classical CV engine that answers in plain language — and never guesses beyond the evidence.

🛰️

Grounded Answers

Every response is backed by real pixel-level measurements, not just a VLM's visual impression — with a confidence score attached.

🔀

Automatic Change Detection

ECC alignment, Lab-space diffing and Otsu thresholding compare any two dates and isolate genuine land-cover change.

🚨

Watchlist Alerts

Save an area as a baseline once. Every future check is compared automatically — with an instant alert if something changed.

How it works

From imagery to insight in four steps

1

Upload Imagery

Up to 16 satellite images — a single scene, or oldest → newest for change tracking.

2

Ask in Plain Language

Hindi or English — describe, compare, measure, locate, or count.

3

Router + CV + VLM Reason Together

Intent detection, land-cover analytics and Gemini reasoning combine on the evidence.

4

Grounded Answer + Alerts

Get a confidence-scored answer, a downloadable report, and ongoing watchlist monitoring.
